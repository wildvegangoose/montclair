Notable people

Painters, astronauts, poets & Hall of Famers

People with ties to Montclair, from Israel Crane, James Howe and landscape master George Inness to Buzz Aldrin, Yogi Berra, Jessie Redmon Fauset and contemporary artists. Each card states the person's tie to town. Where a published source or pre-1951 Montclair city directory gives an address for someone no longer living, you can open that house's story. Living people, and anyone whose time here came after 1950, are listed without addresses. See also neighboring South Orange Through Time.

1774–1858Business & Building

Merchant, Newark–Pompton Turnpike builder and early civic leader who gave Cranetown its name

Built the Federal-style Israel Crane House at 159 Glenridge Avenue in 1796 (moved to 110 Orange Road in 1965).

⌂ Lived at 159 Glenridge Avenue (1796–1858; National Register nomination (house moved to 110 Orange Road in 1965))

James Howe

1775–1850Public Life & Law

Formerly enslaved Montclair resident who became the town's first documented African American homeowner in 1831

Manumitted by Nathaniel Crane in 1817; inherited the c. 1780 house at 369 Claremont Avenue, six acres and $400 under Crane's 1831 will.

⌂ Lived at 369 Claremont Avenue (1831–1850; Nathaniel Crane will (1831); Friends of the Howe House)
