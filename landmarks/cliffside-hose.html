Historic Firehouse · 1901

Cliffside Hose Company No. 4

Upper Montclair's century-old neighborhood firehouse—still sending Engine 2 and Truck 2 out onto Valley Road.

📍 588 Valley Road🏛 National Register of Historic Places (1988)

Cliffside Hose Company No. 4 on Valley Road · JonBaretz / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 3.0

Protecting the growing north end

As Upper Montclair grew from a quiet cluster of Speertown farms into a thriving commuter village in the 1890s, residents pressed the newly incorporated Town of Montclair for closer fire protection. Built in 1901 on Valley Road just south of Bellevue Avenue, Cliffside Hose Company No. 4 gave the north end its own permanent station.

Its residentially scaled masonry facade and hose-bay doors were crafted to sit comfortably between Upper Montclair's shops and tree-lined streets.

More than a century of continuous service

Listed on the National Register of Historic Places on July 1, 1988 as part of Montclair's thematic historic resources survey, the building has never retired from its original mission.

Today 588 Valley Road still operates as an active Montclair Fire Department station, housing Engine 2 and Truck 2.

Did you know?

  • Built in 1901—just seven years after Montclair incorporated as a town.
  • Still an active firehouse (Engine 2 and Truck 2) more than 120 years later.
  • Added to the National Register of Historic Places on July 1, 1988.

Fire-insurance maps

Cliffside Hose Company No. 4 on the Sanborn maps

Sanborn maps were drawn for fire insurers and show every building: yellow for wood frame, pink for brick, blue for stone, with porches, stories and outbuildings.
