Elevated Railroad Station · 1904 (line 1873)

Watchung Avenue Station

Montclair's only elevated rail station—perched on an embankment above Watchung Plaza since 1904.

📍 106 Watchung Avenue (at Park Street)🏛 National Register of Historic Places (1984)✏️ Erie Railroad

Watchung Avenue station on its elevated embankment · Tlantanu / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 3.0

From Park Street to Watchung Plaza

Trains first stopped here on January 1, 1873 as "Park Street" on the brand-new Montclair Railway, right along the old border between Cranetown and Speertown. In 1904 the Erie Railroad built the surviving wooden depot atop an elevated embankment carrying the tracks cleanly over Watchung Avenue—making it the only above-street station in Montclair.

On March 31, 1919 the railroad renamed the stop Watchung Avenue, matching the surrounding Watchung Plaza business district (whose Tudor commercial blocks, including Bradner's Pharmacy, grew up right beside the bridge).

From Boonton Line to Midtown Direct

Added to the National Register of Historic Places on June 22, 1984 as part of New Jersey's Operating Passenger Railroad Stations thematic listing, the timber depot survived the transition from Erie to Erie-Lackawanna, Conrail and NJ Transit.

Until September 30, 2002 every train here ran to Hoboken on the Boonton Line; that morning, the Montclair Connection merged the tracks into the electrified Montclair–Boonton Line with Midtown Direct service to Manhattan.

Did you know?

  • Opened January 1, 1873 as Park Street; current depot built in 1904 and renamed Watchung Avenue on March 31, 1919.
  • The only elevated (non-street-level) rail station in Montclair.
  • Added to the National Register of Historic Places on June 22, 1984.
